01

Recommended first class

Hair-Loss & Scalp Micropigmentation Consultations: A Communication Class

Listen first. Set expectations. Respect the decision.

Barbers, stylists, educators and owners.

  1. Complete a record: goal, routine, questions, limits and next step.
  2. Find and rewrite three unsupported promises.
  3. Explain the agreed next step in 60 seconds.

Participant leaves withA five-part consultation record.

How learning is checked

Five criteria, scored 0–2: listening, relevant questions, clear limits, accurate claims and an agreed next step.

Value for the program

A sensitive topic becomes a practical lesson in professional communication.

The first class begins with listening. A client asks about hair loss, privacy and a maintenance routine. Participants practice a fictional consultation. They record the client's goal, routine, questions, limits and next step. Then they find and rewrite unsupported promises. Success means explaining a clear next step without pressure. This is communication education, not a procedure or a medical diagnosis. The proposed sixty-minute format gives time to model the conversation, practice, receive feedback and explain the decision.

Can the participant explain what the calculation includes, what it excludes and which action they will test?

Value for the program

Participants connect technical work to choices they can explain in their business.

The business direction connects skill to decisions. Participants use a fictional example to distinguish revenue, variable costs and contribution margin. The educator direction brings together craft, identity and responsible artificial intelligence. Start with one skill that a learner can demonstrate. Model it, guide practice and ask the learner to explain the decision. Then review a prepared bilingual draft. Check the facts, the meaning and the promises before using it. 03 / Try a small part of the method

What would you say
to Jordan?

Fictional case: Jordan wants to understand cosmetic hair options. He travels often, values discretion and has questions about maintenance. His suitability for any service has not been established.

Example to challenge

“Perfect forever. A photo is enough to decide. You will not need ongoing care.”
Compare a more responsible response

“Let’s begin with what you want to understand and the routine that works for you. We can clarify the questions, limits, costs and care information that need to be checked before you decide on a next step.”

What makes this useful?

It begins with the person’s goal, avoids a guarantee and leaves room for questions and choice. A useful consultation may end with information, an appropriate referral or time to think.

Use fictional details. Nothing typed here is sent or stored; download before leaving.

03 / Prueba una parte del método

¿Qué le dirías
a Jordan?

Caso ficticio: Jordan quiere conocer opciones cosméticas para el cabello. Viaja con frecuencia, valora la discreción y pregunta por el mantenimiento. No se ha establecido su idoneidad para ningún servicio.

Ejemplo para cuestionar

«Perfecto para siempre. Basta una foto para decidir. No necesitarás cuidados continuos».
Comparar una respuesta más responsable

«Comencemos por lo que quieres entender y la rutina que funciona para ti. Podemos aclarar las preguntas, límites, costos y cuidados que deben verificarse antes de que decidas un siguiente paso».

¿Por qué resulta útil?

Comienza con la meta de la persona, evita una garantía y deja espacio para preguntas y decisiones. Una consulta útil puede terminar con información, una derivación apropiada o tiempo para pensar.

Usa datos ficticios. Nada de lo escrito se envía ni se guarda; descarga antes de salir.
